- **Front building line:** national Ministerial Regulation No. 55 (MR55) §41 and BMA B.E. 2544 Building Control Ordinance §§5(4), 50. `กึ่งกลางถนนสาธารณะ` is the public road centreline, `เขตถนนสาธารณะ` its boundary. Under 6 m, general rule 3 m from centreline. For the taller/covered class, under 10 m use 6 m from centreline, 10–20 m inclusive use 10% of width from near road boundary, above 20 m use 2 m from near road boundary. Keep the tests and local exceptions separate. At Pracha Suk width the triggered near-edge distance is 1.17 m.
- **Height:** MR55 §44 and BMA §49 use twice the perpendicular horizontal distance to the **opposite** boundary of the nearest public road, measured to the applicable building top. Different height datums apply to category classification. MR55 §§45–46 and BMA §49 have special road cases. Treat multi-frontage cases as review until those branches are verified. Water setbacks use MR55 §42 and the public-water boundary.
- **Private plot lines and clear width:** MR55 §50 uses 2 m for façades with openings/balconies at building height ≤9 m, 3 m above 9 m and below 23 m. Clip the stricter applicable condition and keep front road and private boundary geometry distinct. Check abutting qualifying row/shophouses under MR55 §49 and same-owner buildings under §48 when relevant.
- **Open space and access:** MR55 §§1, 33(1) use uncovered qualifying area ≥30% of the largest floor for housing/multifamily. BMA §52(1) specifies 30% of site area for residential use. Apply the latter to multifamily conservatively, flag the classification interpretation, and report both denominators. BMA §§52(6), 53 describe public-road-facing exemption geometry (qualifying façade length >1/8 of exterior perimeter and ≤20 m from road) and alternate open frontage/internal road provisions for the covered buildings. Keep `Needs verification` when surveyed access is absent. - **Categories and detailed review:** Building Control Act §4 sets ≥23 m high-rise and ≥10,000 m² extra-large thresholds. MR55 §1 sets large-building triggers (>2,000 m², or height ≥15 m with area >1,000 m² up to 2,000 m²) and identifies special buildings. MR33 §2 as amended by MR50 §5 has continuous public-road, network and ≥12 m frontage requirements for high/extra-large buildings. ## Geometry experiment and implementation decisions

- Shapely with GEOS 3.14.1 tested a concave metric fixture with a 3 m inset and front/roof cuts. At a 20 m roof the envelope was 420 m² with 11.7 m ROW, 396 m² at 6 m, and 372 m² at 5 m. A 2 m neck split after a 1.1 m inward offset and a 5 m rectangle vanished after a 3 m offset. On the 828 m² fixture, the three-storey wide form totals 840 m² GFA and leaves 548 m² geometrically uncovered. The six-storey stepped form totals 756 m² and leaves 681 m² uncovered. These are synthetic geometry checks with straight parallel road edges.
- Use EPSG:32647 with local origin translation in a browser worker. Separate zoning eligibility, eligible area, floor envelopes, proposed floor polygons, metrics and independent validation. Preserve multipolygons and rings. Use GEOS-WASM with consistent precision and explicit error states. Constrain every slab at roof/top elevation, subtract the union of projected covered areas for open space, and validate each mass against the original rule geometry. Use Vite, TypeScript, React, MapLibre, Terra Draw, Proj4js, Vitest and Playwright. One static deploy, with bounded same-origin datasets.
- First release profiles: detached house up to two storeys/8 m and standalone multifamily below 23 m capped at 1,000 m² GFA to exclude large-building size triggers. The cap is product scope. Start with an L-shaped lower, wider concept opening to a yard and a narrower stepped concept retaining a larger open area. Full report keeps special category, egress, frontage/ROW provenance, parking and detailed permit review visible.
